Privacy

Scoring runs in your browser. Pasted configs and bill exports are not sent to a backend for analysis. Optional cookieless Plausible page/usage events (metadata only) may be enabled at deploy — never paste contents. See each tool’s footer and MONETIZATION.md for the free-forever policy.
