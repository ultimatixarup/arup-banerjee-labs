Playbook Module 1 Terms Privacy

Privacy

The Agentic AI Career Playbook is a static site. There is no signup, no account, and nothing to buy. These pages do not set cookies.

What stays on this device

Module 1 can save your Hands-Off Challenge, Brief Card, and Ownership Log in this browser’s local storage. That text, your Hands count, and your rank are not sent to me or anyone else. I can’t see them or recover them. Scripts on these pages are not allowed to make network requests (connect-src 'none').

Use Clear my data on Module 1 to erase what the playbook saved in this browser. Clearing your browsing data, or using a private window, can erase it too.

Anonymous step counts

When a site code is set, the playbook landing page and the module pages (not this Privacy page, and not the Terms page) may count, anonymously and without cookies: which page you opened, which section came into view, how far you scrolled, and which buttons you used (for example copy, download, volunteer, or a link to another Labs page). The only values sent are fixed labels from a list in the code, such as playbook-m1/step_viewed/challenge or playbook/volunteer_clicked/top.

Those counts never include what you type, your Hands number, your rank, card text, names, email, the page title, the site you came from, or the query string. Nothing identifies you. Browsers that send Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control are skipped, and counts are sent only from the public site, not from a copy on your computer. The request is one GoatCounter image to site code arup-labs. The full list of labels is in the site’s shared/EVENTS.md.

What the host may log

Loading the page does involve a web host. Your browser downloads these pages from GitHub Pages, which, like most hosts, may keep standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security purposes (GitHub’s explanation). I don’t receive those logs.

Copies and exports

Data saved by the activity stays in the browser’s local storage. It leaves that storage only when you copy it, download a bug card, or otherwise export it yourself. Those copies are ordinary text. Store and share them carefully. Don’t put real private information in them.

Who else might see local data

Anyone who can use this browser profile on this device, and browser extensions you’ve installed. On a shared device, use Clear my data before you leave.

What these pages don’t do

  • No account, no email field, and no signup.
  • No advertising.
  • No cookies.
  • No payment.
  • No count of your words, scores, or files. Step counts, when on, are the fixed labels described above.

Volunteer signup happens only by an email you choose to send. This site does not ask for that email in a form, and step counts do not include it.
