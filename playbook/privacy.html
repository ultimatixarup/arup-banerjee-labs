Privacy

The Agentic AI Career Playbook is a static site. It has no analytics, no tracking, no cookies set by these pages, and no signup.

What stays on this device

Module 1 can save your Hands-Off Challenge, Brief Card, and Ownership Log in this browser’s local storage. That data is not sent to me or anyone else. I can’t see it or recover it. The page’s security settings block its code from contacting the network (connect-src 'none').

Use Clear my data on Module 1 to erase what the playbook saved in this browser. Clearing your browsing data, or using a private window, can erase it too.

What the host may log

Loading the page does involve a web host. Your browser downloads these pages from GitHub Pages, which, like most hosts, may keep standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security purposes (GitHub’s explanation). I don’t receive those logs, and these pages do not add analytics on top of them.

Copies and exports

Data saved by the activity stays in the browser’s local storage. It leaves that storage only when you copy it, download a bug card, or otherwise export it yourself. Those copies are ordinary text. Store and share them carefully. Don’t put real private information in them.

Who else might see local data

Anyone who can use this browser profile on this device, and browser extensions you’ve installed. On a shared device, use Clear my data before you leave.

What these pages don’t do

  • No account, no email field, and no signup.
  • No analytics or advertising scripts.
  • No cookies set by the playbook.
  • No payment.

Volunteer signup happens only by an email you choose to send. This site does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.
